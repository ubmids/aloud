# Demo video

The script, shot list and people plan moved to [docs/video/](video/) on
15 October 2026, when Tears of Steel was re-described and the app was run on
the Vega Virtual Device:

- [video/SCRIPT.md](video/SCRIPT.md): 2:50, the hero at 0:06, every part marked
  as needing nobody, Subomi, or a blind or low-vision viewer.
- [video/SHOTS.md](video/SHOTS.md): the shot list and the pre-rendered assets.
- [video/PEOPLE.md](video/PEOPLE.md): who to ask, what to film, consent wording.

The hero lines are from the shipped track (`tv/src/data/films/tears-of-steel.json`):
at 1:36.5 *"Flying ship descends lower."* and at 1:42.4 *"The flying ship
continues descending toward the residential buildings below."*, with the
picture fading up on the flying ship in a rifle scope.

Do not say "live" or "real time"; do not say Menu describes what is on screen
now (it repeats the latest description); do not claim a blind viewer has
tested it unless one did, on camera; do not show Alexa voice commands.
