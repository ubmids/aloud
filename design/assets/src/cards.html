Cooking. Ironing. A toddler on your lap.
A film in your second language.
Eyes that are not what they were.
Or no sight at all.

Ahead of time, not live.

A vision model writes one sentence for each silence.
Each sentence is spoken, measured, and placed inside its gap.
The TV does no AI work at all. A person can review every line.
